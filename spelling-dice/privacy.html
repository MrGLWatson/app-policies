Privacy Policy — Spelling Dice

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Spelling Dice is developed by Gary Watson. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Collect

Spelling Dice does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted:

  • High scores
  • Game history (last 20 games)
  • App settings (language, timer duration, scoring mode)
  • In-app purchase status (tip/donation)

In-App Purchases

Spelling Dice offers an optional tip (in-app purchase). All purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Children

Spelling Dice does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
