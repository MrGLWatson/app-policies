Support — Spelling Dice

How to Play

Shake 16 letter dice and find as many words as possible before the timer runs out. Words must be at least 3 letters long and formed by connecting adjacent letters on the grid.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the language?

Tap the settings icon on the main screen and choose your preferred language/dictionary.

How do I change the timer duration?

In settings, select from 1, 2, 3 or 5 minute game durations.

What is Letter Value scoring?

Letter Value scoring awards points based on the Scrabble value of each letter in the word, rather than the word's length.

How do I look up a word definition?

Tap any word in the results screen or possible words list to look it up in the system dictionary.

What are Possible Words?

After each game, Spelling Dice shows all the words that could have been found on the board that you didn't find.
