FAQs

Q: How do I play?
A: Drag anywhere on the board to slide your cannon along the bottom of the screen, and tap to fire. Clear the formation of aliens before they reach the ground. Open Settings and choose How to Play for the full rules.

Q: Is anything paid for, or are there ads?
A: No. Invadeways is free, with no ads and no subscriptions. The optional tip in Settings is a thank-you and unlocks nothing.

Q: I found a bug!
A: Please email us with details and your device info.

Q: I have an idea for a new feature.
A: We'd love to hear it — please email us and we'll consider it for a future update.

Thanks for playing!
