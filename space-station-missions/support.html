FAQs

Q: I found a bug!
A: Please email us with details and your device info.

Q: I think some mission or astronaut information is wrong.
A: Please email us quoting the mission or person and the detail in question — we review every report and fix confirmed errors in the next update.

Q: The 360° tour or the NASA picture won't load.
A: These come from outside the app (ESA and NASA) and need an internet connection. If one still won't load, please let us know which one it is.

Q: The 3D station is blank or flickers.
A: Please email us with your device model and iOS version so we can look into it.

Q: Incorrect caption, or a photo that doesn't match the person or mission.
A: Please email us quoting the mission or person and the photo's caption, and say what is wrong with it.

Q: I want to tell you something else, or ask a question.
A: Please email us any time. Every message is read, and we are glad to hear suggestions.

Thanks for using my apps!
