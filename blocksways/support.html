FAQs

Q: How do I play?
A: Drag the three shapes from the tray onto the 9×9 grid. Fill a whole row or column and it clears away. A new set of three shapes appears once you have placed all of them. The game ends when none of the shapes will fit anywhere.

Q: How is the score worked out?
A: You score points for every block you place, and more for clearing lines — clearing several lines at once, or on back-to-back moves, is worth a bonus. Your best score is saved on your device.
