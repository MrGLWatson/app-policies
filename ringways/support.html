FAQs

Q: How do I play?
A: Drag any ring round to turn it — it snaps to the nearest segment when you let go. Turn the rings until every spoke, from the centre to the edge, is a single colour.

Q: How are moves counted?
A: Each time you turn a ring to a new position counts as one move. Solve the puzzle in fewer moves to set a new best, which is saved on your device. There is no timer.

Q: What are the Easy, Medium and Hard levels?
A: On Easy, each ring turns on its own. On Medium, turning a ring also turns the ring just inside it the other way. On Hard, turning a ring also turns both of its neighbours the other way. Tap the + button to start a new game at any level, and use Undo if the rings get tangled. Every puzzle can be solved, and your best score is kept separately for each level.
