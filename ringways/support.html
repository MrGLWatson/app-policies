FAQs

Q: How do I play?
A: Put a finger on a ring and turn it round. Each ring has an opening, and rings are held together by connectors with clamps on the end. Turn a ring until its opening reaches the clamp holding it, and it slips free and disappears. Free every ring to clear the level.

Q: Why won’t a ring turn?
A: A ring whose connector is clamped on another ring is locked in place until that other ring has been freed, so free the ring it is holding first. A ring will also stop if it, or its connector, branch or any part of it would hit another ring, connector, branch or stick. Connectors, branches and sticks can pass through a ring’s opening.

Q: Can a ring carry more than one connector?
A: Yes. From level 5 a ring can carry two connectors, and on later levels more. A ring that carries connectors can’t turn until every ring its connectors are clamped on has been freed.

Q: What are the short arms on some rings?
A: They are branches. A branch is fixed to its ring and holds nothing, but it turns with the ring and can’t pass over another ring or connector, so it can stop a ring turning part-way round. Turn the ring the other way, or through an opening, to get round it. A branch disappears with its ring.

Q: What are the sticks with a silver sleeve?
A: A stick slides along its own line through a silver sleeve that stays where it is. A ring can’t turn through a stick, and a stick can’t slide through a ring, though both can pass through a ring’s opening. Put a finger on the stick and slide it along its line. When it comes right out of the sleeve, the stick and the sleeve both disappear and the way is clear. Sliding a stick counts as one move.

Q: What are the sticks with a hook on the end?
A: They are bent sticks. The bend can’t go through the sleeve, so a bent stick only comes out one way, straight end first. Slide it the other way and it stops when the bend reaches the sleeve.

Q: What is the U-shaped stick with two sleeves?
A: A U-shaped stick has a sleeve on each of its two arms and slides straight along the line of its arms. Its base can’t go through the sleeves, so it only comes out base first: slide it towards the base and both arms leave their sleeves together, and the stick and both sleeves disappear. Slide it the other way and it stops when the base meets the sleeves.

Q: Some sticks have no silver sleeve. What holds them?
A: On later levels a ring’s own arm can hold a stick, with the block on the end of the arm acting as the sleeve. That ring can’t turn until the stick has been slid right out of the block.

Q: What is the big closed ring with a stick inside it?
A: A closed ring has no opening, so it can’t slip free the way other rings do, and it can’t be turned. It holds the rings round it with connectors. Free those rings one by one, and when the last has gone the closed ring has nothing left to hold and disappears by itself. The stick trapped inside is shut in by the ring until then; once the ring has gone, slide the stick out of its sleeve to finish the level. A level is complete when every ring and every stick has gone.

Q: Why are some rings bigger?
A: On later levels some rings are larger than others. They work in just the same way, but their connectors and clamps sit further out.

Q: What happens to a connector when the ring it was holding is freed?
A: It stays fixed to the ring that carries it, and now swings loose with that ring. It disappears when its own ring does.

Q: How are moves counted?
A: Each time you put a finger on a ring, turn it and let go counts as one move. Tap Undo to take back your last move. There is no timer. Every puzzle can be solved.
