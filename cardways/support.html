FAQs

Q: Which games are included?
A: Klondike (Draw 1 or Draw 3), FreeCell, and Spider (1, 2 or 4 suits). Switch between them any time from the + menu, and change the Klondike and Spider options in Settings.

Q: How do I play each game?
A: Tap the ⓘ button on the table for a How to Play guide to the game you're playing.

Q: Can every game really be won?
A: Yes. Every deal is checked by a solver before you see it, so there is always a way to win. If you get stuck, undo as many moves as you like, or tap the light bulb for a hint — it will also tell you if there's no way left to win from where you are.

Q: Spider won't deal any more cards.
A: In Spider every column needs at least one card before you can deal. Put a card in each empty column, then tap the stock again.

Q: Is anything paid for, or are there ads?
A: No. Cardways is completely free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Every game and feature is available without any charge.

Q: I found a bug!
A: Please email us with details and your device info.

Q: I have an idea for a new feature.
A: We'd love to hear it — please email us and we'll consider it for a future update.
